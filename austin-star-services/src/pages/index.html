FAQs

Common questions

Do you offer free quotes?{{icon:plus}}

Yes. We offer free quotes on air conditioning and heating repairs, replacements and installations. Call {{site.phone}} or use the form above.

How often should my AC and heating be serviced?{{icon:plus}}

We recommend servicing your heating and cooling system at least twice a year: the AC in spring and the heating in fall.

Should I just get my ducts sealed?{{icon:plus}}

Not always. Sealing alone can make some airflow problems worse if the ducts are undersized. We test first and tell you whether sealing, resizing or both makes sense.

Which areas do you serve?{{icon:plus}}

Austin, Travis County and surrounding areas, including Kyle, Buda, San Marcos and Dripping Springs.

What are your office hours?{{icon:plus}}

Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. For urgent AC or flood problems, give us a call.
